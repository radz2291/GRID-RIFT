One Runner. One rival. A relay worth fighting for.

01 / CONTROL

Click a visible rival once to chase and keep attacking. Right-click ground to move; nearby rivals are attacked when you arrive. A then click ground engages nearby rivals around that point and stops when clear. A then click a rival locks pursuit onto that rival. S stops all orders.

02 / OUTPLAY

Q then click to fire a pulse. W dashes toward your cursor. Ridges block sight and attacks. Shift + drag (or middle drag) pans the camera with a hand cursor. Hold Z / X for a small sideways peek, or C for a higher view; release to return. Retreat to your spawn ring to recover health. Bright terrain is in sight; shaded areas can hide rivals.

03 / HOLD

Stay inside the central ring to capture. Hold it uncontested to reach 600 points.
