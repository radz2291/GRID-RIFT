# Gameplay evolution plan v0.2 · Historical amendment

The owner approved hero-led autonomous warfare and tactical construction option A on 21 October 2026. This record preserves that transition from relay combat. The authoritative consolidated forward design is now [CURRENT-DESIGN.md](CURRENT-DESIGN.md), version 0.3. The current [roadmap](10-DEVELOPMENT-ROADMAP.md) and [handoff](STAGE3A-HANDOFF.md) supersede this amendment's earlier design-only stop after 3A.

Preserved decisions: direct personal hero control; independent teammate heroes; personally owned autonomous forces pursuing high-level intent; predefined construction at valid locations; ongoing economic and territorial pressure; core-based strategic outcome rather than relay-score victory. Keep local deterministic testing, fair information access, modular policies and read-only rendering/evaluation.

Stage 0/1 work and legacy experiment definitions remain evidence. Actual Stage 2 completion must be verified from source, not inferred. The noncombat replacement test remains 3X. The new full cycle includes candidate implementation, independent challenge, bounded repair, re-verification, product playtest, evidence and delivery. It does not retroactively mark any gameplay complete.
