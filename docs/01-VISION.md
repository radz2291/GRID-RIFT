# 01 · Vision · v0.3

Forward direction: [current design](CURRENT-DESIGN.md). Delivered capabilities: [status](STATUS.md).

You fight as a hero and lead an autonomous force in an outdoor technological wilderness. Your attention, incomplete information, resources and commitments shape the next confrontation. A good order specifies the desired outcome; a capable unit discovers how to advance it while circumstances change. Independent teammate heroes can pursue different priorities while cooperating toward a shared outcome.

The intended product combines tactical hero play, strategic investment and structured construction. Build a defense at a crossing, then discover that a rival built an alternate route. Choose whether to relocate, defend your economy or attack their core. The world's ongoing activity must make neglect consequential.

Success means the owner can play, understand a unit's response to one intention, identify a strategic alternative after a loss, and inspect a replay. A developer can change the policy, construction catalog, team structure or victory module without rewriting unrelated core systems.

The approved Stage 1 relay duel remains a useful controls and combat baseline. The original two-lane 3v3 specification remains a historical experiment, not a mandatory product destination. Develop the new direction incrementally through the full-cycle roadmap. Preserve small content, meaningful interaction, readable original outdoor art and honest evaluation. Do not infer emotions or general intelligence from simulated behaviour.
